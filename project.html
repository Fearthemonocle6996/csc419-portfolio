QuickBite

A cafeteria ordering app for students.

Full description

QuickBite lets a student browse the cafeteria menu, add items to a cart and submit an order from their phone. The cafeteria sees incoming orders and prepares them in sequence, so nobody has to stand in a queue to order.

The problem it solved

At peak times the cafeteria queue is long, students lose time between classes, and orders taken by hand lead to mistakes. QuickBite moves ordering online so that each order arrives clearly and in the right order.

Features

  • Browse the menu by category
  • Add items to a cart and change quantities
  • Place an order from a phone in a few taps
  • See an order summary and confirmation
  • Cafeteria view of incoming orders
  • Order status updates from received to ready

What I learned

  • Designing around the user’s path: the fewer taps between opening the app and placing an order, the more likely students are to use it.
  • Structuring data so menu items, orders and order lines stay consistent when many orders arrive at once.
  • Testing on small screens early, because most students would order from their phones.
